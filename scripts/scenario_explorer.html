How these plans were made

One method, different targets

Every plan except today's rule comes from the same large neighborhood search. For the cushion plans the planner was told every window closes 10, 20 or 30 minutes early. For spare capacity it was told trucks hold 51 units instead of 60. The checker then scored each plan against the real windows and capacity.

The traffic test

Thirty saved days of random travel times: a city-wide congestion factor (mean 1.0, CV 0.2) times road-level noise (CV 0.1). Heavy congestion uses mean 1.15, CV 0.3. Each plan is driven exactly as written, and any service that starts after its window, or any truck back after its shift, is a late event.

What the data rule out

  • Fewer than 8 trucks: the 45 orders total 422 units and a truck holds 60.
  • A 9-hour day: customer C039 opens at minute 500, so its truck cannot be back before minute 560.
  • The fewest-miles plan is proven optimal for travel by an exact Gurobi model.
